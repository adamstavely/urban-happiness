Partnering with the enterprise on superintelligence (SI)
Our SI work is built to complement the enterprise's, not compete with it. The architecture is federated by design. The enterprise provides general support to the whole agency and runs the enterprise MCP Gateway. Like other mission partners, we run our own local MCP gateway in direct support of our mission, along with the mission-specific SI capabilities our users need, at the tempo the mission requires.

That federation works best when we work together. We'll coordinate with the enterprise on standards and interoperability, and share what we learn in the mission space. The enterprise has its role, we have ours, and together we deliver SI for everyone.


A federated architecture, built for the mission
Our architecture is federated by design. The enterprise provides general support to the whole agency, with the foundational services and standards everyone relies on. Our office provides direct support to our mission partners, with the capabilities they need to get the mission done, at the tempo the mission requires.
We'll use enterprise capabilities where they meet the mission's needs, and we'll build and run our own where the mission calls for something different. That isn't duplication for its own sake. It's how a federated model is supposed to work. The enterprise has its role, we have ours, and working together where our work meets is how we deliver for everyone.
